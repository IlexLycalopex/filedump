Privacy

Last updated 17 September 2026

This is a personal site, and it is built to collect as little as possible. There is no sign-up, no contact form and no advertising technology on it. The short version: nothing here identifies you, and nothing is sold or shared.

Analytics

Visits are measured using Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is privacy-first and does not use cookies, local storage or device fingerprinting, and it does not track you across other websites. Because no information is stored on or read from your device, and no personal data is collected, this site does not need to ask you for cookie consent.

What is recorded is aggregate and not linked to an individual:

  • Pages viewed and number of visits
  • The referring site or search engine, where your browser provides one
  • Country, browser and device type
  • Page load performance

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google to fetch them. That request carries your IP address, as any web request does. Google's handling of it is covered by their own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Links from this site to mysoftx3.com carry campaign tags in the web address, the utm_ parameters you may see after the question mark. These record only that a visit arrived from this site and which link was used. They contain nothing about you personally. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy and cookie choices apply, not this one.

Your rights

No personal data is collected here, so there is nothing held about you to access, correct or erase. If you have a question about this note, the quickest route is LinkedIn.

Changes

If the tools used on this site change, this note will be updated and the date above revised.
